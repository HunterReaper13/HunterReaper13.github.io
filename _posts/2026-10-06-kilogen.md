Currently, Kilogen remains as my long term indie project with all the **physics** in the game being **mostly custom** allowing for planets with different **drags, gravities and effects on the movement system** of the game preventing *wallrunning, sliding or enhancing jump height, speed or thrust strength*. This project is far from complete but still impressive nonetheless.

</div>

</div>

<div class="media-row" markdown="1">

<div class="text" markdown="1">

As well as all the physics stuff, Kilogen remains an impressive art piece reflecting my skills as a **3D artist** with the animations being *"on twos"* and the models being all **made by me**. As well, the shaders in unity were made by me using **Unity Shader Graph** but I inted to gain more control by programming it later with **HLSL**.
