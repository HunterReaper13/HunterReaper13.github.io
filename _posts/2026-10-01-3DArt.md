As well as programming, I've been developing **3D art cinematics** and making models for my games in **Blender** for **over 3 years**, this has given me a greater understanding of the artist workflow and the designers workflow with my own **GDD's** and **Trello boards** to help organise myself through my personal and university projects.

In **Blender**, I've spent hours creating fun **shading styles** and utilising **color themes** to present a feeling with each art piece which has helped me understand *"game feel"* with my projects while also learning **Blender's shader graph** has helped me create shaders for **Unity** and **Unreal** while animating in Blender has transferred my skills to using the Unity and Unreal **animator tools** with **IK/FK switches** and rigging with weight painting.

But, I still have lots to learn within these specialties and definitely need to spend a lot more time to truly master them but with my focus mainly being on programming I've only been learning these skills when I don't have access to a designer or artist to rely on for personal projects.
