Cave Divers 2 sticks out as my first try at procedural generation, which although my assignment was just to create a **procedurally generated dungeon** with **random walk** or **binary space partitioning**, I decided to take a different path that still fit the criteria with **cellular automata**.

</div>

</div>

<div class="media-row" markdown="1">

<div class="text" markdown="1">

To create the caves, I started with a randomly generated **noise map** to create a random map then with **cellular automata** I created the *caves* through iteration before ensuring that every tile is explorable by defining regions through **Dijkstra's algorithm** and clearing out irrelevant caves that are too small and creating *lakes* using big sections of wall tiles that are surrounded by ground. Then by using the distance between edge tiles I connected the caves up with **pythagoras and drawing circles** around each tile to widen the corridor.

After creating the procedural generation, I created AI pathfinding within the caves using **A* algorithms** to create enemy patrolling and chasing states, then created randomly generated chests, hearts that increase stats creating a really simple **roguelike loop** with each new cave getting larger and more full of enemies and loot. But with this project being an old one it still stands out as one of my favourite projects to work on and got my head around **algorithms, generation and optimisations**.
